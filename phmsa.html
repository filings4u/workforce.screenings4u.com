THE PHMSA FRAMEWORK

Covered pipeline functions come with specific testing duties.

PHMSA testing requirements are established in 49 CFR Part 199, with DOT testing procedures in 49 CFR Part 40. Part 199 applies to operators subject to pipeline safety regulations under Parts 192, 193 or 195.

Review PHMSA's official program overview →
WHO AND WHAT IS COVERED?

Identify the people performing covered functions.

Coverage follows regulated functions, including applicable contractor work—not just a job title.

01

Pipeline operations

Employees and contractors performing covered operations functions on regulated pipelines.

02

Maintenance activities

Personnel performing covered inspection, maintenance and related regulated functions.

03

Emergency response

Individuals conducting covered emergency-response functions on pipelines or LNG facilities.

04

Operator and contractor records

Operators remain responsible for applicable compliance duties even when contractors perform covered work.

WORKFORCE / RANDOM SELECTIONS
2026 PHMSA RANDOM TESTING RATE

Know the difference between drug and alcohol testing.

PHMSA has set the minimum annual random drug testing rate for covered employees at 50% in calendar year 2026. PHMSA does not require a corresponding program of random alcohol testing under Part 199.

Read the official 2026 notice →

IMPORTANT COMPLIANCE DISTINCTION

Software supports your program—it does not replace PHMSA obligations.

Operators must maintain applicable anti-drug and alcohol-misuse prevention plans, follow required testing procedures, and meet any reporting and recordkeeping duties. Workforce is an administrative software tool, not a PHMSA approval or certification. Testing and collection services may have separate charges.
