COMMON QUESTIONS

Employer software, explained.

Answers to help you choose the right Workforce subscription for your operation.

Is Workforce Employer software a monthly subscription?

Yes. Employer Essential, Professional and Enterprise are monthly software subscriptions. Individual testing or fulfillment charges may be separate.

Can Workforce support DOT and non-DOT programs?

Yes. Workforce plans provide applicable DOT and non-DOT workflow support, with capabilities dependent on your selected tier and configuration.

How many employees can my plan manage?

Essential supports up to 100 employees or drivers; Professional supports up to 500. Enterprise uses an expanded-capacity configuration.

What happens after I choose a plan?

You continue to the established secure checkout. After successful payment, you follow the account creation and onboarding process.
