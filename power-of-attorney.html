Verifying payment with Workforce…

1. Principal — the person or organization granting permission

Use the exact legal identity of the employer, owner-operator, motor carrier, or C/TPA that purchased the subscription.

2. Who may act for the Principal?

Select the agent or agents authorized under this document. Workforce and your designated C/TPA are separate agents, and each is limited to the powers you choose.

No agent may use the Principal's Login.gov password, MFA codes, or recovery credentials. FMCSA, state, and municipal agencies may require their own approved representative delegation.

3. Specific powers you are granting

Choose only the tasks that you authorize. Unselected powers are excluded. Financial transactions and legal representation are not granted unless separately authorized.

For any task not listed above, request a separate written authorization describing the specific power before Workforce or a C/TPA acts on your behalf.

4. Review legal terms and sign electronically

Your electronic signature will be displayed in cursive, but the underlying saved consent record, signed document version, and server timestamp are the evidence of your agreement.
