FTA PROGRAM REQUIREMENTS

Public transit testing comes with specific responsibilities.

FTA drug and alcohol program requirements are addressed in 49 CFR Part 655, with DOT testing procedures in 49 CFR Part 40. Coverage includes specified recipients of federal transit funding and contractors performing covered safety-sensitive functions. View official FTA guidance →

WHO NEEDS TO BE ORGANIZED?

Know who performs covered safety-sensitive work.

Coverage is based on the functions performed and the applicable funding and contracting arrangements—not merely an employee's title.

01

Transit vehicle operators

Employees operating revenue-service vehicles, including when not in revenue service.

02

Vehicle maintenance personnel

Workers maintaining revenue-service vehicles or equipment used in revenue service.

03

Dispatchers & controllers

Personnel controlling the movement or dispatch of revenue-service vehicles.

04

Security & CDL functions

Covered armed security duties and other safety-sensitive duties defined by the rule.

Contractor and subcontractor employees can also be covered when performing safety-sensitive functions. Read FTA contractor guidance.
