SOFTWARE & SUBSCRIPTIONS

Choosing a subscription

Who is Workforce for?

Workforce provides separate software products for employers managing their own workforce, C/TPAs administering client programs, and FMCSA owner-operators managing eligible single-driver operations. Explore all solutions.

What are the starting software prices?

Employer software starts at $85 per month, C/TPA software starts at $125 per month, and FMCSA Owner-Operator software starts at $74.95 per year. Pricing and included capabilities depend on the selected tier. Compare all plans.

Do I need to choose a customer type before purchasing?

Yes. Choose the product based on your actual responsibilities, then compare its plans and follow the checkout link on the product or pricing page.

Can I use Owner-Operator software for an FAA, FRA, FTA, PHMSA or USCG program?

The current Owner-Operator subscription is specifically described for FMCSA single-driver motor-carrier programs. Other regulated operations should review Employer or C/TPA software as appropriate.

TESTING & COMPLIANCE

Managing the work

Does a software subscription include every laboratory test?

No. Testing, collections, background screenings and other fulfillment services may be separately charged. Check the chosen plan and checkout for what is included.

Can Workforce guarantee DOT compliance?

No. The software can help organize workforce records, testing programs, selections and administrative activity, but employers and program administrators remain responsible for applicable regulatory duties.

Does Workforce replace the FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse?

No. The Clearinghouse is a separate federal system with its own required registration, queries and reporting. Visit the official Clearinghouse.

Where can I learn about agency-specific requirements?

Start with our DOT Compliance Overview, then select the FMCSA, FAA, FRA, FTA, PHMSA or USCG guide. Always consult the official agency's current regulations.
