THE NEW ENTRANT PROCESS

Organize the documents before the audit.

FMCSA generally conducts the New Entrant Safety Audit during the first 12 months of operations. Workforce offers preparation and support; only FMCSA or its certified safety auditors conduct the official audit.

01

Understand your requirements

Identify the FMCSA safety controls and recordkeeping requirements that apply to your carrier.

02

Prepare the records

Organize driver qualifications, testing program records, insurance, maintenance and operating documents.

03

Review readiness

Receive the preparation services included in your selected package so you can address outstanding items.

AUDIT READINESS

Records that may be reviewed.

FMCSA's Safety Audit Resource Guide identifies document categories that carriers should be prepared to present. Actual requests depend on the operation.

01

Carrier & insurance

Authority, required financial responsibility, safety management controls and carrier information.

02

Driver qualifications

Driver lists, licenses/CDLs, medical qualification, MVRs and other qualification records.

03

Hours of service

Duty-status records, ELD information where applicable, and supporting documentation.

04

Vehicles & maintenance

Vehicle lists, inspection reports, maintenance files and repair documentation.

05

Drug & alcohol program

Required testing policy, consortium participation, test documentation and relevant Clearinghouse compliance.

06

Crash & hazardous materials

Accident registers and hazardous materials records when applicable to your operations.
