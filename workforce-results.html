WHY REPORTING MATTERS

What happens after a test is just as important.

For teams coordinating workforce testing, access to the right result and supporting record is essential for follow-up. When those details are spread across multiple systems, review and reporting take more time than they should.

THE EVERYDAY CHALLENGE

Less searching. More clarity.

A connected software workflow helps make results and supporting records easier to find and use responsibly.

01

Scattered result records

Testing outcomes can be difficult to locate when information is shared through different channels.

02

Disconnected workforce context

Understanding which employee, driver, employer, or program a record belongs to matters.

03

Time-consuming reporting

Administrative reviews become harder when teams have to reconstruct activity manually.

04

Follow-up visibility

Teams benefit from a consistent view of relevant work and the records supporting it.
